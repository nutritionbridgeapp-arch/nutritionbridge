NutritionBridge — User Agreement

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. Purpose

NutritionBridge is a utility application that transfers nutrition records from Android Health Connect to HUAWEI Health using HUAWEI Health Kit.

2. User authorization

The application accesses and transfers health information only after the user grants the permissions requested by Android Health Connect and HUAWEI Health Kit. The user may refuse or revoke permissions at any time.

3. User responsibility

The user is responsible for reviewing records and permissions before authorizing a transfer. NutritionBridge is a data-transfer utility and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice.

4. Accuracy

NutritionBridge transfers nutrition values available from the source record and maps them to fields supported by HUAWEI Health Kit. Differences between source and destination schemas may affect which fields can be transferred.

5. Third-party services

The transfer depends on Android Health Connect and HUAWEI Health/HUAWEI Health Kit. Availability and behavior may change according to their providers, permissions, policies, and regional availability.

6. Health data

NutritionBridge does not sell users' nutrition data and does not use it for advertising.

7. Changes

This agreement may be updated when functionality or applicable requirements change.
