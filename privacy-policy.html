NutritionBridge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. Data processed

With the user's explicit authorization, NutritionBridge accesses nutrition records available in Android Health Connect, including, where provided: food/meal name, meal type, date and time, energy/calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, and other nutrition fields supported by the source record and HUAWEI Health Kit schema.

2. How the data is used

Nutrition data is used solely to perform the transfer requested by the user from Health Connect to HUAWEI Health. The application does not use nutrition data for advertising, profiling, sale, or unrelated analytics.

3. Where the data goes

NutritionBridge processes records locally on the user's Android device and sends selected nutrition records to HUAWEI Health only after the user grants the required HUAWEI Health Kit authorization. NutritionBridge does not operate a server or cloud database for users' nutrition records.

4. User control and permissions

The user decides whether to grant Health Connect and HUAWEI Health Kit permissions and can revoke them through the relevant settings.

5. Data retention

NutritionBridge does not intentionally retain a cloud copy of nutrition records. Temporary in-memory processing may occur during synchronization.

6. Security

Health data is accessed and transferred only through authorized Health Connect and HUAWEI Health Kit APIs.

7. Third-party services

The application uses Android Health Connect and HUAWEI Health Kit/HUAWEI Health. Their respective terms and privacy policies apply to those services.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when functionality or applicable requirements change.

9. Contact

For privacy questions regarding NutritionBridge, contact nutritionbridge.app@gmail.com.
